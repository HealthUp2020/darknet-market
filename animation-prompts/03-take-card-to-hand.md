# Animation — take card (market → hand), Everdell-style

Prepend the shared spec from [00-everdell-motion.md](00-everdell-motion.md), then this prompt.

```
[MOTION LANGUAGE] + Animate TAKING a card from the market into the operator's hand. On click the
card lifts (anticipation), arcs down-and-across to the fanned hand, growing a soft shadow in
flight, then tucks into the hand fan with a gentle settle while the neighboring hand cards ease
apart to make room; meanwhile the emptied market slot briefly dims and a replacement card arcs in
from the deck to refill it. Show a market row + a hand fan + a "TAKE" trigger (click a market card
to take it). Cards use the existing NIGHT MARKET GameCard look.
```
