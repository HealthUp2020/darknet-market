Mobile / portrait reflow

Zones stack vertically in reading order: turn banner pinned top, then a collapsed rival strip, the market, the prices board, and the operator zone pinned to the bottom as a persistent action bar. The 7-card market row and the hand fan become horizontally swipe-scrollable lanes (snap per card) so they never shrink below legible size. The three rival panels collapse into one compact horizontal strip of avatar + CR + active-dot chips, tap to expand. The terminal becomes a slide-over drawer behind the log-toggle icon. The action dock docks to the safe-area bottom; the drone stepper opens as a small popover on Exchange. Everything else keeps its exact tokens, glow rules, and type — only the arrangement changes.
