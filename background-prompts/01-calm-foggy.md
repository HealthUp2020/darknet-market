# Background — Option A: "Calm & Foggy"

Prepend the shared spec from [00-background-spec.md](00-background-spec.md), then this prompt.
Mood reference: the foggy dystopian cityscape — restrained, premium, quiet.

```
[SHARED SPEC] + DENSITY: CALM & FOGGY. Lean heavily on HAZE and NEGATIVE SPACE. Thick volumetric
fog rolls through the corridor; the far skyline and architecture are dissolved into soft silhouettes.
Neon is SPARSE — a few orange faction sigils and one or two cool cyan signs glowing through the mist,
everything else muted grey-teal. The center pocket is deep and near-empty, almost meditative. Rain is
a fine drifting mist rather than hard streaks. This is the safest for card readability and reads the
most "premium / high-end black market." Minimal, moody, expensive.
```
