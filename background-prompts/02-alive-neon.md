# Background — Option B: "Alive & Neon"

Prepend the shared spec from [00-background-spec.md](00-background-spec.md), then this prompt.
Mood reference: the Kowloon-style rain-soaked neon street — dense, vibrant, alive.

```
[SHARED SPEC] + DENSITY: ALIVE & NEON. Lean into a living STREET-MARKET density in the FAR layers:
stacked neon signage (invented glyphs + faction sigils), hanging cables, steam vents, market-stall
glow, drones weaving between buildings, and warm window lights up the corridor walls. Rich orange +
cyan neon reflected hard in a wet street. KEEP THE CENTER POCKET DARK AND CLEAR — all this life is
pushed to the top, sides, and deep background, behind heavy atmospheric fade so it never fights the
cards. Steam and rain are heavier and more animated. This is the most vibrant / "there's a whole city
out there" option — the trade-off is it's the busiest, so lean on depth-blur and vignette to protect
the play area.
```
